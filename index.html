THE CORE INNOVATION

Barrel CAM Mechanism

Traditional umbrellas use a sliding-runner mechanism unchanged since Alexander Ege’s 1880 patent. The Umbrella Pandemonium replaces it with a barrel CAM — a stationary helical-grooved cylinder that converts shaft push into radial rib rotation, eliminating pinch-point hazards and distributing wind load evenly across all six ribs.

The mechanism draws directly from NASA’s StarShade coronagraph satellite, which uses precision petal deployment to block starlight for exoplanet observation. We adapted the two-phase origami unfurl sequence for an everyday consumer product.
